About me

Greetings! I'm Soman Singh,

a seasoned 3D Artist, Graphic and Web Designer with extensive expertise in crafting designs and graphics across various mediums such as corporate branding, advertising, social media, print, and digital platforms. With over 7+ years of experience, I’ve played a pivotal role in numerous creative projects, contributing my adept, dynamic, and innovative creative and communication skills.

My proficiency spans from conceptualization to execution, encompassing graphic design, brainstorming, and visual conceptualization. Leveraging a blend of artistic, technical, and design acumen, I specialize in enhancing visual appeal and adding a distinctive edge to every project I undertake.

As a Graphic Designer, I firmly believe in employing a creative approach to problem-solving, utilizing lateral and parallel thinking to ideate innovative concepts and designs. I stay abreast of evolving market trends, ensuring that my work remains fresh and relevant.

I eagerly anticipate engaging in diverse projects that allow me to showcase my creativity, proficiency in design software such as Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, as well as my expertise in social media, layout design, branding, and more. My goal is to contribute to elevating brand identities and assisting organizations in achieving their objectives through my excellent communication, time management, and training skills.

Professional Skills: Adobe Photoshop | Adobe Illustrator | Adobe InDesign | Social Media | Layout Design | Magazine Design | Brochures | Flyers | Posters | Logos | Brand Identity | Infographics, and more!
